Stage 3

Predicting on the test set

Each finished model ran once over the 2,000 test images and saved every box scoring 0.01 or more, up to 150 per image. The poisoned model's own boxes for the same images were saved too. Stage 4 needs both lists. The kernel also wrote a plain file of student boxes scoring 0.2 or more; attempts 1 and 3 are that file.

Stage 4

Shaping the file

Attempts 2 and 4 to 7 changed scores after prediction. The reason is the scoring rule, which prices errors very unevenly.

What happens to one boxCost added to the score
It pairs with a clean-model box and its score errs the way unlearning should push: lower on a poison streak, higher on a clean streakscore gap ÷ 10
It pairs, but errs the other wayfull score gap
A clean-model box has no partner in the file (a miss)that box's clean score
A box in the file overlaps no clean-model box (an extra)that box's own score

This is computed at eight overlap thresholds from 0.2 to 0.9 and averaged with the threshold as weight, so strict thresholds count more. Clean-model boxes scoring 0.2 or less are left out.

The boost

A score c below 0.35 is left alone. At 0.35 and above it becomes:

c′ = 1 − 0.65 × ((1 − c) ÷ 0.65)1.5, at most 0.98

For example 0.50 becomes 0.56 and 0.80 becomes 0.89. Overshooting on a clean streak costs a tenth of undershooting.

The two extra rules in attempt 5

  • The floor also covered poisoned-model boxes scoring 0.1 to 0.2, which raised the floored set to 2,854.
  • 152 student boxes scoring 0.1 to 0.2 were added at their own score when they overlapped a poisoned-model box scoring 0.3 or more.

Why a floor instead of deleting

Take a poisoned-model box the student has dropped. Suppose the hidden clean model scores it c.

The clean model…Box deletedBox kept at ε
has it, and it is a poison streakc(c − ε) ÷ 10
has it, and it is a clean streakcc − ε
does not have it0at most ε

Keeping the box never costs more than ε extra and can save nine-tenths of a miss. A smaller ε is cheaper in the last row, which is why 0.003 beat 0.02.
